SEED PROTECTION - what to do on the server
==========================================

Seed crackers work out your world seed from things every player can see. DonutREP already replaces the hashed seed in the join / respawn packets (seed-protection.yml).
The rest has to be set in the server's own files (a restart is needed). Nothing makes the seed impossible to find for somebody who walks to many structures and
writes down the coordinates, but these steps stop the usual tools.

1. STRUCTURE SEEDS - the best way (Paper)
   config/paper-world-defaults.yml:
     feature-seeds:
       generate-random-seeds-for-all: true

   Or by hand in spigot.yml (world-settings -> default). Use your own numbers, a different set on every server. This block was made for you just now:

     world-settings:
       default:
         seed-village: 1929354369
         seed-desert: 821810259
         seed-igloo: 582270075
         seed-jungle: 92510847
         seed-swamp: 1732405135
         seed-monument: 774971471
         seed-shipwreck: 480689282
         seed-ocean: 1006857454
         seed-outpost: 959405244
         seed-endcity: 503266188
         seed-slime: 600057577
         seed-nether: 1322838085
         seed-mansion: 21556506
         seed-fossil: 5128301
         seed-portal: 1557539117
         seed-ancientcity: 713761145
         seed-trailruins: 1199575129
         seed-trialchambers: 1516276485
         seed-buriedtreasure: 958018954

   Only chunks that are not generated yet use the new numbers; structures that already exist stay where they are. The cleanest result is a new world.

2. ANTI-XRAY (ore positions) - DonutREP switches this on by itself (seed-protection.anti-xray: true) and keeps the old file as paper-world-defaults.yml.before-donutrep; restart once.
   config/paper-world-defaults.yml:
     anticheat:
       anti-xray:
         enabled: true
         engine-mode: 2

3. THE WORLD SEED ITSELF
   Do not use a seed people know or can guess (not a word, a date or a short number). A long random number is best (server.properties -> level-seed, before the world is made).

4. KEEP THE SECRET
   The 'salt' in seed-protection.yml makes the fake seeds. Do not share the file. Changing it changes every fake value (nobody notices).
